06 Good to Know

Things Sellers Always Ask

How do I receive feedback after showings and open houses?

We follow up directly with every buyer's agent after private showings and log visitor counts and impressions from each open house right here on your dashboard.

What happens once we accept an offer?

Once executed, the contract moves to the title company, the buyer delivers earnest money and option fee, and we navigate the option period, inspection, and appraisal through to closing.

What do I need to bring to the closing table?

Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, any remaining unit keys, fobs, garage remotes, and your wire instructions for receiving your net proceeds.
